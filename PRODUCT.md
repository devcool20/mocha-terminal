# Practice Lab

An independent concept feature within Mocha's visual ecosystem. Audience: Indian traders learning US-stock perpetual mechanics, and the Mocha product team reviewing the concept.

## Platform

web

## Goal

Configure a trade, understand its downside and costs, practise the full lifecycle, and share an honest simulated result. Match Mocha's identity with fast, accessible product interactions. High fidelity, working paper-trading functionality and transparent assumptions are required.

## Defaults

Four US stocks; INR display; separate scenario and public-reference wallets; ₹1,00,000 virtual starting funds. No real funds, private integrations or user accounts. Laptop and desktop web only, with reduced-motion support. Minimum workspace width 1120px.
