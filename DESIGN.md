# Mocha product identity

Inspired by the live mochatrade.com site as inspected on November 2, 2026: warm near-black background, copper-orange actions, hairline warm borders, serif editorial accents, compact trading controls.

Scene: an Indian trader at an evening desk, comparing a trade's possible outcomes in a focused, low-light environment. Use restrained orange against layered charcoal; green and red communicate direction and results.

The interface uses the exact Geist Regular, Geist Medium, and Geist Mono Regular files served by mochatrade.com. The official SVG wordmark supplies the brand typography. Source Serif 4 is available from the same source, but the terminal has no editorial hero or decorative serif captions. Fonts and brand assets are served locally. Use 150–220 ms state transitions with reduced-motion alternatives.

The chart is the primary workspace. Laptop and desktop layouts place markets to the left, trade controls to the right, and the ledger below. The compact scenario tape plots each preset's actual model path; it is not a grid of decorative cards. Minimum workspace width is 1120px. No avatar, motivational headline, illustrated sidebar, or mobile navigation. Functional SVG glyphs are drawn on a consistent 16px grid; company marks come from Simple Icons. Every visible action must work. Tooltips disclose model details, chart overlays distinguish existing-position risk from a draft, and all generated results say simulated.
